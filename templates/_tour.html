{# 4. Freed seats #}
Seats freed up in 1 showtimenow

Dune: Part Three IMAX, Sat 19 Dec 20:40: +2 seats in your areas, 9 free now. 2 together in the preferred area: row 6, seats 20-21

In a movie's settings, set how many seats you need together. You get a push when a freed seat lets your group sit together in your areas. Two or three sit in one row; four or more can also sit two rows deep, one directly behind the other.
